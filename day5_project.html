Project Findings¶

The dataset contains 8 employees from four departments.

Key Findings¶

  1. Finance has the highest average salary at INR 90,000.
  2. Chennai has the most employees, with 4 out of 8.
  3. Employee salaries range from INR 30,000 to INR 90,000.

Recommendations¶

  1. Review salary differences between departments.
  2. Use a larger dataset for better salary comparisons.

Limitations¶

The dataset contains only 8 employees, so the findings may not represent a larger company
